FIRE-SAFETY INSIGHTS FROM NASA FLIGHT DATA

Open on the orb. In microgravity there is no rising plume, so a burning fuel droplet wraps itself in a sphere of blue flame. Flame in Freefall takes NASA’s microgravity combustion record, 1950 flight tests from 19 investigations plus 27 NASA technical reports, and answers the question a mission designer actually asks: what do we know about fire in my cabin atmosphere? This deck walks through the live product in four steps: choose, rank, explain, estimate. Then it shows the moment the system refuses to answer.
