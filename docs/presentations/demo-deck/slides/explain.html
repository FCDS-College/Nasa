Then the dashboard says what the flight tests actually measured in that atmosphere. Every number carries the IDs of the NASA table cells it came from, one click from the source row. Example: in FLEX, 94 percent of 48 droplet flames below 16 percent oxygen self-extinguished, versus 8 percent of 24 at 30 percent or more. Each card is labelled: measured, model estimate, or coverage gap. The coverage-gap cards matter most. For pre-EVA air, no solid-material test in BASS-II was flown at 30 percent oxygen, and the dashboard says so instead of staying silent.
