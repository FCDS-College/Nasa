FLAME IN FREEFALL · LIVE DEMO

Now we give it Apollo 1. On 27 January 1967 the cabin held pure oxygen at 16.7 psia during a ground test. NASA later wrote that the cabin materials had been exposed to an atmosphere in which they had never been tested. The system refuses: none of the 1950 flight tests cover this atmosphere. Its oxygen is 2.5 times the highest ever flown, and it is at Earth gravity, while every test was in microgravity. It shows the nearest real test, ACME CFI-G 21162A1 at 40 percent oxygen, and makes no estimate. Then it lists what changed after the fire, each line linked to a NASA page, and points to where data does exist: Orion’s 30 percent atmosphere, with 8 real tests.
