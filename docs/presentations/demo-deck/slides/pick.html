FLAME IN FREEFALL · LIVE DEMO

The product starts from the designer’s question, not from an experiment list. Pick one of five presets or type your own oxygen fraction and pressure. Before any ranking, the system gives a coverage verdict. ISS air, the pre-EVA campout, Orion’s maximum-oxygen design and an Altair-class lunar lander all have flight tests nearby. Apollo 1’s pure-oxygen pad atmosphere does not: it sits two and a half times beyond the highest oxygen ever flown. Note the wording: covered by data, never “safe”.
